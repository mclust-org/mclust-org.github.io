# Acidity data

Acidity index measured in a sample of 155 lakes in the Northeastern
United States. Following Crawford et al. (1992, 1994), the data are
expressed as log(ANC+50), where ANC is the acidity neutralising capacity
value. The data were also used to fit mixture of gaussian distributions
by Richardson and Green (1997), and by McLachlan and Peel (2000, Sec.
6.6.2).

## Usage

``` r
data(acidity)
```
